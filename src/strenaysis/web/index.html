Intro - product brief

Before the query, frame the work.

Strenaysis makes the hidden framing layer of analytical work visible: what question matters, why it matters, and what evidence would make the answer useful.

The Pitch

AI tools have learned to answer questions. Most teams still struggle to frame them.

Strenaysis takes a raw business question, classifies the analytical problem, generates a node-by-node plan, and turns the reasoning into a reviewable workplan.

Mission

Raise the floor on framing questions, not just producing answers.

The mission is to catch vague or misframed asks before analysis begins, so teams spend effort on questions that deserve the work.

Vision

Make the data team's reasoning visible, reviewable, and reusable.

The vision is a shared artifact where stakeholders, analysts, and leads can see the decision, evidence path, assumptions, and workplan in one place.

Riskiest Assumption

Will a working analyst actually trust an LLM's breakdown of their problem?

Trust means the classification feels right, the node sequence is defensible, and the coaching questions are sharp enough to use. The guardrail is structure, not chat.

Problem Space

Three failures that compound silently.

i.

Asks arrive vague.

"Why is churn up?" is a symptom, not an analytical plan.

ii.

Decision context is missing.

Teams need the decision, deadline, and mind-changing evidence before results arrive.

iii.

Unknown unknowns stay hidden.

The riskiest gaps are the ones nobody thinks to ask about.

Who It Helps

Three audiences, one shared frustration.

01

The stakeholder

Operators, PMs, and executives who need to turn a worry into an actionable question.

02

The analyst

DAs and DSs who spend too much time decoding requests and negotiating scope.

03

The lead

Analytics leaders who need reasoning to be legible before work is greenlit.

Prototype Learnings

What changed the product brief.

i.

Response types are the spine.

Facts, assumptions, and hypotheses make review sharper.

ii.

Nodes should be reorderable.

Analysts work non-linearly and should start where the unknowns live.

iii.

The artifact is the product.

The LLM is the muscle. The reusable reasoning artifact is what travels.

Problem framing is the highest-leverage step in any data project. Get it right and every other step compounds. Get it wrong and no analysis recovers.
